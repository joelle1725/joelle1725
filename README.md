<details>
<summary><b>Capstone deep dive: how the chatbot was built</b></summary>

<br/>

**What we built:** An AI chatbot with built-in observability and governance (AI-SIEM) for highly regulated industries, where trust and compliance decide adoption. Containerized on Oracle Cloud, with a FastAPI backend, ChromaDB vector database, RAG for grounded answers, and OpenTelemetry + Splunk for monitoring.

**My contributions**
- Framed the product's value proposition for regulated industries
- Translated sponsor, user, and business needs into product requirements and user stories
- Debugged and iterated on chatbot responses, tightening RAG grounding so answers drew on verified knowledge
- Defined success metrics and the usability validation plan used before launch
- Shaped the storytelling for sponsor and stakeholder reviews

**What I learned**
- **Accuracy alone isn't enough for AI products:** monitoring, safety checks, and explainability are what earn trust.
- **Iteration is everything:** when the model didn't consistently use retrieved context, testing and logging were how we found and fixed it.

**Next steps:** improve RAG reliability, expand the observability dashboards for real-time monitoring, and explore a multi-tenant architecture to serve multiple clients.

</details>
